Why an address and not a ZIP code? One ZIP can sit in two or three districts. Your address is used once to look up your districts and polling places, then thrown away. It's never stored or logged.

What you'll get

Where and how to vote

Your nearest polling places, early-voting sites and drop boxes, plus official links to check your registration and see your full sample ballot.

Who's paying

For your U.S. House and Senate races: money each campaign raised and from whom, plus Super PAC spending for or against them. Straight from FEC filings.

What's been fact-checked

Published fact checks of each candidate's claims from independent fact-checkers, in the fact-checker's own words.

What we can and can't cover yet

Thousands of local election offices each publish their ballots their own way, and there's no free source that lists every race. We say where the gaps are instead of hiding them.
