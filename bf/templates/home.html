We don't keep your address

  1. When you press the button, your address goes to the US Census Bureau (to find your districts) and Google's election data (to find polling places).
  2. We build your page from the answers.
  3. The address is thrown away. It isn't saved, it isn't in any log, and it isn't in the page's link; the link only names your districts.

Why not just a ZIP code? One ZIP can sit in two or three districts, and polling places depend on your street. You can check all of this: the site's code is public.

What you'll get

Where and how to vote

Your nearest polling places, early-voting sites and drop boxes, plus official links to check your registration and see your full sample ballot.

Who's paying

For your U.S. House and Senate races: money each campaign raised and from whom, plus Super PAC spending for or against them. Straight from FEC filings.

What's been fact-checked

Published fact checks of each candidate's claims from independent fact-checkers, in the fact-checker's own words.

What we can and can't cover yet

Thousands of local election offices each publish their ballots their own way, and there's no free source that lists every race. We're adding states one at a time from official sources, and we say where the gaps are instead of hiding them.
